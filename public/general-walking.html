Estimated kcal = (MET − 1) × 3.5 × weight in kilograms × minutes ÷ 200. The presets reproduce the observed General Walking estimates at 165 lb. You can enter a measured calorie value in the editor.

Activities selected by speed from the exercise catalog use their published Adult Compendium walking values.
